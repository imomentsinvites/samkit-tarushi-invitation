VIDEO LOADING:
The first opening video is prepared before the envelope is shown. Event videos are not all
forced to download at startup. The parents/event video is preloaded as the visitor approaches
its section, and nearby event videos are prepared ahead of scrolling. This avoids downloading
all large videos on mobile while reducing the chance of a blank video when a visitor reaches it.
Keep videos reasonably compressed (H.264 MP4, 1080x1920, AAC audio) for mobile playback.
